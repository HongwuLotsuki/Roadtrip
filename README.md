# Roadtrip

A 3D driving and survival game made in Unreal Engine 5.

**▶ Play:** [Download the Windows build from Releases](https://github.com/HongwuLotsuki/Roadtrip/releases/tag/1.0.0)

## Concept

Drive a truck across three regions, keep your hunger, thirst and health up, search the roadside with a roll of the dice, and help the people you meet by returning their lost belongings.

## My role

Solo project: design, Blueprint programming, level setup and builds.
